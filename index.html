About Me

I am an astrophysics PhD researcher at the National Centre for Nuclear Research (NCBJ) in Warsaw, Poland. I began my PhD in October 2025 and work on low-surface-brightness galaxies and their relationship with the cosmic web, with a particular interest in large astronomical surveys.

I completed my bachelor's degree in Physics and master's degree in Astrophysics at the University of Potsdam. My earlier research focused on stellar astrophysics, especially eclipsing binary stars. My interests also include cosmology, scientific computing, and the methods we use to make sense of astronomical data.

PhD Research

Current

Low-Surface-Brightness Galaxies in the Cosmic Web

I investigate how low-surface-brightness galaxies are distributed and how they relate to their environments, using large imaging surveys and statistical methods such as galaxy correlation functions.

METAR Decoder

Planned

A Python tool that turns coded aviation weather reports (METARs) into understandable plain-English descriptions, including wind, visibility, clouds, and weather conditions.

Planned features: paste a METAR, decode it, and read a natural-language explanation.

Chess Game Narrator

Planned

A Python project that reads chess games in PGN format and describes the moves in everyday language.

Planned features: PGN upload, move-by-move narration, and translations into other languages.
